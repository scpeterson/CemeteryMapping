# ADR 0098: Add the Weitzel monument at an estimated location

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-10-15

The owner authorized adding the later-installed Weitzel companion monument north of fixed TLC-HS-0500 using the best available estimate from IMG_6597, IMG_6598 and IMG_6599. Photos identify Jerry Lee (September 2, 1945) and Shirley Jane (October 21, 1950–November 12, 2022). These are inscription details, not independent interment evidence.

Migration 458 adds standalone TLC-HS-0577 approximately eight feet due north of TLC-HS-0500, preserving every existing monument and gravesite. The three camera GPS positions average about 8.3 feet north of the reference point; aligning to the reference longitude and rounding to eight feet is a provisional operational choice, not a measured offset or a triangulated result. Camera headings from opposite sides are consistent with a monument in the same general area, but GPS horizontal errors are about six meters. The owner identifies the reference monument's shadow at the left edge of IMG_6597; missing monument height, ground scale and terrain measurements prevent a reliable shadow-derived distance. Do not portray this as a surveyed position.

Use low data confidence, needs-review status, an explicit ESTIMATED LOCATION note, and source properties containing the offset method, source filenames/hashes, photo timestamps, camera positions/headings and accuracy. Material/type/scope follow the photographed upright gray granite companion monument; condition is unknown. Preserve Jerry's blank death date by recording only visible inscription text. No burial records, gravesite polygons or associations are created without confirmation.

Reject missing reference geometry, inconsistent reference-grave assignment, identifier/name collisions, missing lookups, or a point coincident with another active marker. Environments without the imported reference marker are unchanged. Back up DEV before applying. Verify the eight-foot northward offset, low-confidence review metadata, and byte-for-byte unchanged existing records. Recovery uses the pre-migration backup; automatic rollback is empty.
