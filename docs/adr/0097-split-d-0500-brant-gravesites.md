# ADR 0097: Split D-0500 Brant burials into separate graves

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-10-15

The owner confirmed only Clifford Cornell Brant belongs in D-0500. Bonnie Louise Brant needs a separate grave immediately north; both share TLC-HS-0500, whose surveyed point must remain unchanged.

A split centered on the monument would shift Clifford's polygon south into Jacoueline Lois Brant's D-0499A. Instead, migration 457 preserves Clifford's original grave and monument records unchanged and creates D-0500A (TLC-GPS-0500-01) immediately north of the original northern boundary. Bonnie's estimated polygon extends 10 feet east and 4 feet north, matching existing operational dimensions and avoiding active neighbors. Her existing burial moves to the new grave, with identity and burial details preserved. The shared monument remains linked to Clifford's grave and gains a spanning link to Bonnie's. These estimated polygons do not establish surveyed interment limits.

Reject unexpected burial assignments, missing shared monument or burial links, occupied new identifiers, invalid source geometry/dimensions, and any overlap with active graves. Environments without active D-0500 are unchanged. Back up DEV before applying; verify unchanged monuments, Clifford's complete original record and burial, neighboring geometries (including D-0499A), separate burial assignments, new grave dimensions, and shared monument links. Recovery uses the backup; automatic rollback is empty.
