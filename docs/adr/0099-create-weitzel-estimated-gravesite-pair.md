# ADR 0099: Revise the estimated Weitzel location and add two gravesites

Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-10-16

The owner approved Jerry Lee Weitzel south and Shirley Jane Weitzel north, and explicitly accepted revising estimated TLC-HS-0577 to approximately 11 feet north and 8 feet west of fixed TLC-HS-0500. The initial eight-foot due-north estimate cannot accommodate a standard eastward couple layout without overlapping Bonnie Brant and Martha Traut. The revised point and two 4-by-10-foot polygons avoid these existing graves. The westward offset is not independently supported by the photos; this is an approved provisional operational layout requiring field measurement, not improved survey accuracy.

Migration 459 creates D-0577 (TLC-GPS-0577) for Jerry south and D-0577A (TLC-GPS-0577-01) for Shirley north, with the revised monument at their shared western corner. Both graves extend east and retain estimated geometry and needs-review status. Preserve the original monument point, estimate metadata and review notes in its source-properties history; retain the existing photo evidence and inscription. Link the monument to both graves, with the south grave as primary.

Gravesite assignments do not establish interment, ownership or reservation. Create no burial or ownership records. Keep both grave statuses needs_review so they are not advertised as available. Existing surveyed monuments and all existing graves and burials remain unchanged. Guard against unexpected monument associations, missing reference data, occupied new identifiers, existing-grave overlaps, invalid polygons and coincident monument points. Environments without the Weitzel marker are unchanged. Back up DEV before application and verify position, dimensions, ordering, retained evidence/history, links and unchanged existing records. Recovery uses the backup; automatic rollback is empty.

## Owner correction, 2026-10-16

The owner withdrew the eight-foot westward offset and explicitly requested moving the monument and both graves eight feet east and one foot south. Migration 460 translates all three together, preserving the Jerry-south/Shirley-north arrangement and existing links. It retains prior coordinates and review text in source-properties history and supersedes the old offset in the current estimate. The revised position is approximately ten feet north of TLC-HS-0500. This placement overlaps the estimated polygons for Bonnie Louise Brant and Martha E Traut; flag the conflicts on the Weitzel graves and monument for field verification without moving neighboring records or altering burials. The overlap is a mapped-boundary conflict, not a determination of physical interment locations.
